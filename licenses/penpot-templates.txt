Changes made for Aakara: boards were converted to Aakara layers, groups and nested boards
were flattened, embedded photos and images were replaced with placeholders, fonts were
mapped to Google Fonts, text keeps one style per text box, and Penpot and Kaleidos logos,
the Penpot mascot and Penpot links were removed from the slides template.
